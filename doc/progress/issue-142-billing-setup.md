# Issue 142 billing setup

## Audit

- Customer categories are persisted in Customer.type, validated in customer.validation.ts and edited and filtered through the Customer feature. The job card displays the category. The existing customer category migration converts old vendor types to Others, preserves the Vendor party status and derives ownCompanyVehicle from In-House for customers. The migration backup on the configured database contains zero records with a legacy Vendor type.
- SparePart.partFlag stores source O, L, A, F, U or R. The pricing category, including N, is separate. Reports resolve tax category from reportTaxCategoryCode or the legacy taxCategory values A, B and C. Billing uses the resolved category and the Taxable field.
- JobBillingService and parts-vat.ts calculate customer-paid parts, labour, service charge, discounts, VAT and branch round-off. The service stores invoice totals, VAT breakdowns and the Kia and Non-Kia split. InvoiceDetailPage and the counter sale screen and print already display their stored heads. Bills with historical aggregate-only parts retain the existing summary.
- JobBillingService can consolidate eligible vehicle jobs from the same branch with the same billing customer. It preserves the JobCardLine payer checks before aggregating charges. The existing discovery view groups and pages jobs in the database. The current payer model records Customer, Warranty, Goodwill and Free, or Company at the service level. It has no insurance allocation.
- The opening worksheet records an estimated parts, oil, labour and service breakdown. The signed estimate revision previously saved the sum of priced scope lines before VAT and round-off. Advance preparation and the job card advance summary use the saved estimate amount, with an advisory 60% default. Billing applies eligible advances to the saved invoice total. Delivery checks outstanding active invoices and credit approval, then allocates from GATE_PASS. The job card prints the pass using the recorded delivery details.

## Changes and rollout

- Estimate creation and revision use the existing billing calculator and store the tax policy and rounded total. The signed print shows VAT and round-off. Bill preview and creation use the saved estimate policy, and new bills store their applied policy and exemption reason. Existing invoices and estimate amounts stay as stored. For an in-flight estimate without a VAT snapshot, billing continues the earlier parts VAT treatment. A revision uses the settings in effect when it is created.
- Company-paid estimates retain their existing quote behavior. Estimate lines represent quoted customer scope for customer-paid services. Billing continues to separate payers using actual charged lines. A single-job bill with a new snapshot cannot exceed the approved amount. Consolidated billing uses a common approved policy. If policies differ, staff can bill jobs separately or obtain new approval for updated estimates.
- Government parts exemption uses the government vehicle flag first, then the Government category. Labour and service tax stay as configured. The default rates for spares, oil and accessories are 7.5%. Legacy Fleet maps to Corporate. Source O is Kia Spare. Sources L, A, F, U and R are Non-Kia Spare.
- The administrator preflight uses the billing category resolver for every part with a retail rate, covering parts that can enter an estimate even if they have never been sold. The configured database currently has 176 such parts without a supported category and parts VAT is off. The settings API rejects activation until the list is empty. Operational enablement remains pending catalogue classification.
- Multi-job discovery and job-bill list, preview and creation allow cross-branch access for general managers. Other staff must have an assigned branch. Existing permission gates remain in use. Billing Manager and General Billing Manager inherit Billing Officer permissions.
- The settings page reuses PageHeader, Field, NumberInput and Button. The preflight adds loading, retry, empty and blocked activation states. The estimate print reuses the current NGN formatter and follows its existing print layout. The gate pass logic is unchanged.

## Completion review

- A later read-only preflight found 182 unclassified retail parts. The existing part types are 178 Spares, 3 Oil and 1 Accessories. Spares is the schema default, so a stored S does not prove that someone classified the part. Auto-classifying these records needs a confirmed mapping or a reviewed list before activation.
- The vehicle view already has an Insurance label. The job-card charge model has no insurance value or insurer account allocation. A display-only tag and an insurance payer that changes the invoice are different requirements. The billing treatment needs a business decision before this can be marked complete.
