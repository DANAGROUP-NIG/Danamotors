# Quick guide: workshop supervisor

You give each job to a technician and a bay, watch the workshop on the board, and do the quality check. You own the start of step 2 and step 6.

## The workshop board

1. Open **Job Cards** in the menu. You see the **Board**: one column per step.
2. Each card shows the plate, the customer request, who has it and how long it has been there. Amber means the step is close to its time target, red means it is over.
3. Use the filters at the top for advisor, technician or date. Press **Table** to see the same jobs as a list.

## Step 2: assign the technician

1. Open a job in the Diagnosis column that says "Waiting for a technician to be assigned".
2. Read "What the customer asked for".
3. Choose a technician. Each one shows **Available** or **Busy**.
4. Choose a **Bay**. Each bay says "(free)" or which job is in it.
5. Add a note for the technician if needed. Press **Assign and send to** (the technician's name).

You can still press **Change technician or bay** until the findings go to the engineer.

## Step 6: quality check

1. Open a job in the Quality check column.
2. Mark every work item **Pass** or **Fail**.
3. Tick the general checks, enter the road test **Start km** and **End km**.
4. To fail: mark the item **Fail**, press **Fail and return to technician**, write what must be fixed, press **Return to technician**.
5. To pass: sign the certification ("Vehicle checked and certified as roadworthy in all respects") and press **Pass quality check**. The service advisor gets the job.

## Tablet use

The quality check is made for a tablet in the workshop. Every button is big enough for a finger. The page does not scroll sideways.
