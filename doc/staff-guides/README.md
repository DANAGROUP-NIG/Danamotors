Words used in every guide:

- **Job card**: the record of one visit of one vehicle, from the moment it arrives until it leaves.
- **Step**: the stage the job is at. There are 8: Check-in, Diagnosis, Warranty, Estimate, Repair, Quality check, Billing and Delivery.
- **With**: the line under the job number that says who has the job now. Only that person can work on the step. Everyone else can look but not change it.
- **Estimate**: the list of work and parts, with prices, that the customer approves before work starts.
- **Supplementary estimate**: a second estimate for extra work found during the repair.
- **Advance**: the part payment the customer makes before work starts.
- **Gate pass**: the slip the security post checks before the vehicle leaves.
