# Quick guide: warranty officer

You decide who pays for each item on a job that may be under warranty. You own step 3.

1. Open **Job Cards** and look for jobs with **With: Warranty officer**. You get a notice when one arrives.
2. Open the job. At the top you see the vehicle's warranty (5 years or 100,000 km), the service history and any open campaigns.
3. The service history shows each warranty year by quarter. The rule is a service at least every 3 months or 5,000 km, about 4 a year.
4. If the customer serviced elsewhere, add it with the stamp from the service book: dealer name, date and mileage. Removing a record needs a reason.
5. Under "Items to decide", choose who pays for each item: **Warranty**, **Customer pay**, **Goodwill** or **Campaign**.
6. If the service history is not regular and you choose warranty or goodwill, write a reason. It saves by itself ("Reason saved").
7. Press **Return to service advisor**. The advisor prepares the estimate with your decisions.

Settings for the rule are under **Settings**, **Warranty**, **Service history rule**.
