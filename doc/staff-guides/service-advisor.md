# Quick guide: service advisor

You meet the customer, check the vehicle in, prepare the estimate, bill and hand the vehicle back. You own steps 1, 4, 7 and 8.

## Every day

1. Open **Job Cards** in the menu. The table shows every job; the **With** column says who has each one.
2. Jobs waiting for you have your name in **With**. Open one by clicking its job number.
3. The big heading says the step, for example "Step 4 of 8: Estimate". The button at the bottom right is the next thing to do.

## Step 1: Check-in

1. From the booking, press **Convert to job card**. Scroll the terms to the end, tick the box and press **Continue to job card**.
2. Enter the **Mileage**, the promised date and time, and press **Open job card**.
3. **Details**: check the customer requests. Write instructions for the technician. Press **Continue to walk-around**.
4. **Walk-around**: choose the fuel level, tap the car picture to mark damage, check the 25 items (or press **Mark the rest present**), add photos. It saves by itself ("Saved 10:42").
5. **Customer consent**: hand the screen to the customer. They choose **Owner** or **Representative**, tick the terms and sign. Press **Sign and accept**.
6. Press **Send to workshop supervisor**.

## Step 4: Estimate

1. When the job comes back to you, press **Prepare estimate**. Check quantities and the advance.
2. Press **Present to customer** and hand over the tablet. The customer can **Decline** any item, then ticks "I approve the work above at the prices shown", signs and presses **Approve and sign**.
3. If the customer approves by phone, use **Record phone approval** instead. They sign for it at delivery.
4. If an advance is needed, press **Notify cashier**. When the advance is received, press **Release to workshop**.

## During repair

When the technician finds something new, a banner says "New finding from ...". Press **Prepare supplementary estimate**, then present it or record a phone approval. Approved work carries on meanwhile.

## Step 7: Billing

1. Press **Mark ready and notify customer**. Choose **Phone call** or **SMS** (the app records the call; it does not send SMS yet).
2. Billing staff create the invoice and record the payment. When it is paid, press **Send to delivery**.

## Step 8: Delivery

1. Show the customer the work done and anything declined.
2. Tick each belonging returned. Choose who is collecting. If it is not the owner, a manager must approve.
3. The customer signs. Press **Complete delivery and print gate pass**. The gate pass prints.

## If something is grey

A grey button always says why underneath, for example "Ask the cashier to record the advance." Do what it says, then try again.
