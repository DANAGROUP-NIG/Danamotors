# Quick guide: cashier

You take the advance before work starts and the payment before the vehicle leaves. Anyone with the receipt permission can do this.

## Advance (step 4)

1. The service advisor presses **Notify cashier**; you get a notice with the job number.
2. Open the job and press **Receive advance**.
3. Enter **Amount received (NGN)** and choose the **Mode**: Cash, Bank transfer, POS or Cheque. Cheques cannot be used as advances until they clear.
4. Press **Save receipt**. The screen says "Advance received and reserved for job ...". The advisor can now release the job.

## Invoice and payment (step 7)

1. When the vehicle is ready, open the job. Press **Create invoice** on the Invoice card.
2. If storage charge is due (more than 48 hours after the customer was told, ₦2,000.00 a day), accept it or remove it with a reason.
3. Press **Create invoice**. The card shows the invoice number and **Unpaid**.
4. Press **Record payment**. The amount due is filled in. Choose the **Mode** and press **Save receipt**.
5. The card shows **Paid**. The service advisor can now send the job to delivery.

Money always shows in naira with two decimals, for example ₦152,112.50.
