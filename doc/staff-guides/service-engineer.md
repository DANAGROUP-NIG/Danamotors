# Quick guide: service engineer

You check the technician's findings before the job goes on. You own the end of step 2.

1. Open **Job Cards** and look for jobs with **With: Service engineer**. You also get a notice when one arrives.
2. Open the job. The heading says "Service engineer is reviewing the findings".
3. Read each finding, the work and parts the technician added, and any photos.
4. If something is flagged "Possible warranty", the screen says "This job goes to the warranty officer next."
5. If the findings are right, press **Confirm and send to warranty officer** (or **Confirm and send to service advisor** when nothing may be warranty).
6. If something must be checked again, press **Return to technician**, write what to check in the note and press **Return to technician**. The note is required.

The printed job card shows the confirmed findings in the "Cause and nature of defect" box, as on the paper card.
