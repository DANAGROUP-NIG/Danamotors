# Quick guide: technician

You find what is wrong, do the approved work and collect parts. You use a phone. You own part of step 2 and step 5.

## My jobs

1. Open **My jobs** in the menu. Jobs are under **Today**, **Waiting parts** and **Done**.
2. Each job says what to do next. Tap it to open.

## Step 2: findings

1. Read the note from the workshop supervisor.
2. For each customer request, write what you found in "What did you find?".
3. Press **Add work** and **Add part** to add what is needed. You never see prices.
4. If it may be under warranty, switch on **Possible warranty**.
5. Add a photo if it helps.
6. Found something the customer did not mention? Press **Add a finding the customer did not mention**.
7. It saves by itself. Press **Send to service engineer**.

If the engineer sends it back, their note is at the top. Fix it and send again.

## Step 5: repair

1. **Your work** lists each approved job. Press **Start**, then **Done** when finished. Done records your time and labour.
2. Parts ready at the store say so. Press **Collect and sign**, tick the parts you took, sign and press **Sign and collect**.
3. Need another part? Press **Need a part**, say why, choose the part and press **Send to service advisor**. Do not start that work until the customer approves it.
4. Waiting for a part? Press **Pause job**, then **Resume repair** when it comes.
5. When everything is done, press **Send for quality check**.

## Returned from quality check

The job comes back with "Returned from quality check" and the supervisor's note. Only the failed items need redoing. Press **Start**, **Done**, then **Send for quality check** again.
